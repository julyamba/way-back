How it works

Three taps between you and the way home.

Start recording when you set off. When you want to return, WayBack turns your route around and leads you along it.

Record

Pick Walking, Vehicle or Boat and tap START. Your route draws on screen as you move, using GPS outdoors and step counting when the signal is gone.

Mark

Drop a marker at any spot worth remembering: a fork, a parked car, a doorway. Add a note or a photo so you recognise it on the way back.

Guide back

The map stays still and the arrow turns with you. Follow the trail, watch the distance fall, and get a heads-up before each turn and marker.

Features

Made for places where the signal gives up.

Everything runs on the phone. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing needs a connection.

GPS first, steps when it's gone

Outdoors, WayBack follows GPS and ignores poor fixes. Indoors or under cover it switches to counting your steps and reading your direction from the phone's sensors, so the trail keeps growing.

Hold the phone however you like

Carry it flat in front of you, swinging at your side, or in a pocket. WayBack notices how the phone is carried and keeps tracking your walking direction.

An arrow that faces where you face

The map stays north-up while the arrow turns with your body, so lining up with the trail is obvious.

Markers with notes and photos

Pin the spots that matter. The guide shows the next marker and how far away it is.

Recording that survives a crash

The route is saved every ten seconds. If the app or the phone stops, your recording is recovered on the next launch.

Trails that outlive the app

Choose a backup folder and every trail is mirrored there. Reinstall, pick the folder again, and they are all back.

A map you can handle

Pinch to zoom, twist with two fingers to rotate, double-tap to reset. The guide view stays centred on you.

Height changes

On phones with a pressure sensor, WayBack tracks climbs and descents and tells you what is ahead on the way back.

Good to know

Straight answers.

WayBack is a helper for getting back to where you started. It is not safety equipment, so in remote or risky places carry proper navigation gear as well.

Does it need internet or an account?

No. There is no sign-in and nothing is sent anywhere. Trails, notes and photos stay on your phone.

Does location have to be switched on?

For the best trail, yes. WayBack asks you to turn it on when you tap START. Without GPS it still records by counting steps, which is less exact over long distances.

Will it work indoors, in tunnels or in caves?

It keeps recording by step counting and compass direction. That works well over short distances and drifts over long ones, and metal or rock can disturb the compass. Use markers at every junction.

What about running, driving or boats?

Running is recorded like walking. Vehicle and Boat modes rely on GPS only, so they need open sky. It does not work underwater.

Why don't I see height changes?

Height tracking uses the phone's air-pressure sensor. Many phones do not have one, and the height tiles stay hidden on those.

What happens to my trails if I uninstall?

Turn on “Keep trails after uninstall” in Settings and choose a folder. Trails are copied there and restored when you connect the same folder after reinstalling.

Install

Ready when you head out.

WayBack is installed from an APK file. It needs Android 8.0 or newer.

Download the APK View the source
  1. Download the latest app-release APK to your phone.
  2. Open it and allow installing from this source when Android asks.
  3. Allow location, activity and notifications, then tap START.
